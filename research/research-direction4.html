04 · HPC Particle Methods

HPC-Based Particle Method Development

Scalable particle algorithms make strongly nonlinear multiphysics simulations practical on modern GPU and multi-GPU computing platforms.

Research focus

I develop particle-based computational methods for fluids, solids, fracture, and granular materials. The core formulations include smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH), peridynamics (PD), and the discrete element method (DEM), together with coupling strategies that exchange forces, momentum, heat, and damage across their interfaces.

The numerical work spans formulation, discretization, solver design, GPU implementation, load balancing, verification, and performance analysis.

Particle formulations

  • SPH for thermal-fluid dynamics
  • PD for deformation and fracture
  • DEM for granular and debris behavior
  • SPH-PD-DEM multiphysics coupling

HPC foundations

  • GPU-parallel algorithms
  • Multi-GPU domain decomposition
  • Dynamic load balancing
  • Matrix-free iterative solvers
