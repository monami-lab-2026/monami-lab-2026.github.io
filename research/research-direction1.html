01 · Severe Accidents

Digital Twins for Severe Accidents

Particle-based simulation offers the geometric flexibility and multiphysics capability needed to reproduce complex, evolving severe-accident phenomena.

Research focus

I develop computational frameworks that can represent severe-accident scenarios as evolving virtual counterparts of the physical system. The work focuses on phenomena that are difficult to capture with fixed computational meshes, including large deformation, free surfaces, fragmentation, material relocation, and structural interaction.

By combining high-fidelity particle methods with validation data and scenario-specific models, these digital twins can support mechanistic interpretation, safety assessment, and the exploration of accident-management strategies.

Core methods

  • Smoothed particle hydrodynamics
  • Multiphase and highly viscous flow modeling
  • Fluid-structure and particle-structure coupling
  • Verification and experimental validation

Target phenomena

  • Corium spreading and relocation
  • Impact, fragmentation, and debris behavior
  • Steam-explosion loading
  • Core-catcher and containment response
