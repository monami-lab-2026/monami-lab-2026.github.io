03 · Advanced Reactors

Digital Twins for Advanced Reactors

Multiscale simulation connects dynamic system behavior with high-fidelity component models to build predictive virtual representations of advanced reactors.

Research focus

I integrate system-level models with higher-fidelity descriptions of the reactor core, heat transport system, and energy-conversion components. This approach captures plant-wide dynamics without losing the local physics needed for design and safety decisions.

The resulting digital-twin framework is intended to support design-space exploration, transient analysis, control development, and model-informed operation of emerging reactor concepts.

Modeling scales

  • Plant and system dynamics
  • Component-scale thermal-fluid models
  • High-fidelity reactor-core analysis
  • Consistent exchange across scales

Applications

  • Advanced reactor design studies
  • Operational and control transients
  • Heat transport and power conversion
  • Virtual commissioning and safety analysis
