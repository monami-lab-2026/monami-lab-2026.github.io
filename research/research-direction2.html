02 · High-Fidelity Analysis

High-Fidelity Computational Analysis

Detailed analysis of complex local phenomena using particle-based methods and high-performance computing.

Research focus

Nuclear systems involve local and complex physical phenomena that system analysis or low-dimensional models cannot represent adequately. High-resolution numerical analysis is needed to understand phenomena with large spatial and temporal variations, including multiphase flow, free-surface evolution, melting and solidification, rapid heat transfer, and severe accidents.

MONAMI Lab develops and continuously extends a particle-based, high-fidelity numerical analysis platform for these phenomena. Centered on Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH), we employ and couple different particle-based methods according to the governing physics and analysis objectives to simulate complex flows, heat transfer, and multiphysics behavior effectively.

Because high-resolution analysis is computationally demanding, we actively use GPU parallel computing and high-performance computing technologies. This enables efficient simulation of larger domains at higher spatial and temporal resolution and provides a foundation for expanding applications to diverse reactor thermal-hydraulic and accident phenomena.

Through collaboration with research groups at Seoul National University, Kyung Hee University, and other institutions, we plan to expand particle-based numerical analysis and nuclear thermal-hydraulics research and advance high-fidelity analysis technologies for a broader range of physical phenomena.
