02 · Reactor Multiphysics

High-Fidelity Reactor Multiphysics Analysis

Coupled neutronics and thermal-hydraulics models resolve the feedback mechanisms that govern reactor behavior during steady operation and rapid transients.

Research focus

I study reactor multiphysics by coupling neutron transport and thermal-hydraulic solvers across complementary levels of fidelity. The objective is to preserve system-scale context while resolving local power, temperature, density, and flow feedback where it matters.

This work emphasizes robust data exchange, spatial and temporal mapping, solver synchronization, verification, and physically interpretable transient analysis.

Coupled physics

  • Neutron transport and kinetics
  • System and three-dimensional thermal hydraulics
  • Fuel and structural temperature feedback
  • Power-transient analysis

Computational priorities

  • Consistent multiphysics interfaces
  • Multirate time integration
  • High-fidelity local feedback
  • Verification against reference solutions
