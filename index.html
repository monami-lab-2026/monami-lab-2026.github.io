MONAMI Lab

Multiphysics Of Nuclear Analysis, Modeling and Innovation

Our Mission

We develop predictive computational technologies for nuclear systems by integrating reactor physics, thermal hydraulics, structural mechanics, and high-performance computing.

MONAMI connects high-fidelity multiphysics, multiscale system modeling, and particle-based methods to create reactor digital twins grounded in physical interpretation, verification, and validation.

Our goal is to build reliable tools for advanced reactor analysis, severe-accident research, and next-generation nuclear engineering innovation.

Research directions

High-fidelity computational science for reactor digital twins.

Our research integrates particle methods, reactor multiphysics, multiscale modeling, and high-performance computing to develop predictive digital twins for nuclear systems.

01 · SEVERE ACCIDENTS

Digital Twins for Severe Accidents

Leveraging the flexibility of particle-based methods to explore diverse severe-accident scenarios, including multiphase interaction, corium behavior, spreading, fragmentation, and fluid-structure interaction.

02 · REACTOR MULTIPHYSICS

High-Fidelity Reactor Multiphysics Analysis

Coupling neutronics and thermal hydraulics across system, subchannel, and three-dimensional models to resolve local feedback and transient behavior with high physical fidelity.

03 · ADVANCED REACTORS

Digital Twins for Advanced Reactors

Integrating multiscale and system-level modeling with high-fidelity component models to build predictive simulation environments for advanced reactor design and operation.

04 · HPC PARTICLE METHODS

HPC-Based Particle Method Development

Developing scalable smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH), peridynamics (PD), and discrete element methods (DEM), together with robust coupling strategies among these formulations.

Software

SOPHIA

A Lagrangian-based HPC-CFD code for versatile thermal-hydraulics and nuclear safety application

NEA-1911 · OECD-NEA Official Repository

Description: SOPHIA is a GPU-enabled, particle-based multiphase flow solver built on smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH), Lagrangian discretization, and matrix-free iterative solvers. Designed for high-fidelity thermal-hydraulic analysis of corium spreading, severe accidents, free-surface dynamics, highly viscous multiphase systems, and fluid–structure interaction.

Principal Investigator: Prof. Eung Soo Kim
Co-Developers: Y. B. Jo, S. H. Park, H. Y. Choi, T. S. Choi, S. S. Park, H. S. Yoo, Y. L. Ahn, J. H. Kim, T. H. Lee, H. Chae, J. M. Park, J. R. Kim, D. H. Kim
